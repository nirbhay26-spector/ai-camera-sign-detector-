How to Perform the "Signal for Help"

The International "Signal for Help" is a silent, one-handed distress gesture recognized worldwide.

1
Open Palm
Raise hand facing camera with palm open and thumb extended outwards.
2
Tuck Thumb
Fold thumb inward across the center of your palm. Four fingers remain up.
3
Close Fingers
Fold your four fingers down over the thumb, forming a fist enclosing the thumb.
💡 Tip for Demo: Hold the closed fist steady for 2.0 seconds until the confirmation meter reaches 100%!
